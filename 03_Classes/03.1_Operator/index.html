    Operator

    A knife is the most powerful form of Artifice, second only to a gun.

    An Operator excels at weapon use, forgoing fancy powers for strict adherence to tactical domination. Trick opponents with Misdirection, and cash in EP for massive Harm.

    Core Features

    FEATURE Misdirection

    Once per Allegiance Turn, when you declare an Offensive Move and your target declares their Defensive Move, you may spend 1 EP to trigger Misdirection.

    Cancel your declared Move. You immediately recover the Action Slot and any EP spent to declare it. The target cannot change or cancel their declared Defensive Move.

    Using the recovered Action Slot, you must declare a different Offensive Move against the target. This new Move automatically triggers its Exploit effect.

    After the Clash resolves, the new Offensive Move becomes Exhausted. You may only have one Move Exhausted by Misdirection at a time. If you Exhaust a new Move this way, your previously Exhausted Move instantly recovers.

    Repertoire

    Manage your 8-Move Repertoire limit carefully between Rests.

    Fight Moves

    OFFENSE Neutralize 2

    DIE: 4 PWR: 1 DMG: 4

    Exploit: Each Win reduces the target's DIE by 1 on their next Clash, to a minimum of 1. If DIE cannot be reduced any more, reduce PWR by 1 instead.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • Increase DIE by 1.
    • Decrease EP by 1.

    Rank 2

    • Increase DIE by 1.
    • Gain Area 1.

    Rank 3

    • On 2 Wins, next Win is an automatic Break.
    • On Break: Reduce the target's DIE by half on their next Move. If the Move would deal 0 Harm, gain 1 EP.

    OFFENSE Shock and Awe 3

    DIE: 5 PWR: 3 DMG: 3

    Exploit: This Move gains Afflict Tremor 3.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • Add Vicious.
    • Increase DIE by 1.

    Rank 2

    • Increase Afflict by 1.
    • Decrease EP by 1.

    Rank 3

    • Add Area 1, but decrease DIE by 1.
    • If the target's DIE is reduced for this Clash, this Move gains Area 1.

    DEFENSE Weave Through 1

    DIE: 4 PWR: 1 DMG: 0

    On Win: reduce the next instance of Harm taken by 1.

    Innate

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • Add Deflect
    • Add Reinforced

    Rank 2

    • Increase DIE by 1.
    • Reduce the next instance of Harm taken by an additional 1.

    Rank 3

    • Add Exploit: this Move gains "On Win: all subsequent dice gain +1 DIE."
    • Add Exploit: this Move gains "On Loss: Reduce the DIE of the opponent's next die by 1."

    PASSIVE Cold Start

    You begin each Fight Scene with 4 EP instead of the standard 3 EP.

    Once per Allegiance Turn, when you score at least 1 Win on a Move with an active Exploit effect, gain 1 EP.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • Begin each fight scene with 5 EP instead.
    • Increase your maximum EP to 8.

    Rank 2

    • Gain 2 EP instead.
    • Grant an ally of your choice 1 EP as well.

    Rank 3

    • Once per Fight Scene, you may use Misdirection at no EP cost.
    • You can now use Misdirection twice per Allegiance turn. It costs +1 EP the second time it's used in an Allegiance turn.

    Hustle Moves

    UTILITY Well-Connected 2

    You have contacts that can get items for you. Once per Chapter, you may use this Move to purchase an item less than 2,000 Sal that's illegal or regulated in your current area. It has a 25% markup if purchased this way, though this markup doesn't apply to the cost limit. This Move is unusable in the Depths.

    UTILITY Field Strip 3

    You may use this Move to add or remove a Mod from a Weapon without a Check or Jury-Rig Kit.

    TRAIT Concealed Carry

    You may perfectly hide a one-Handed weapon of an Anomaly Tier half your own (min I) on your person. It is undetectable by standard visual inspection or pat-down.

    TRAIT Conditioned

    Get +1 to your Grit for all narrative Checks to withstand torture, drugs, exhaustion, or environmental effects.

    Subclasses

    Unlocking your first move in a subclass locks you into that path.

    Chain

    A barrage of gunfire erupted from the stranger's coat. When the smoke cleared, all that was left of Wallace was a mass of flesh.

    Chains excel in volume of fire and suppression, devastating targets with high Dice Moves and pressuring them with status application.

    Chain Features

    FEATURE Barrage

    When you trigger Misdirection, you may spend an additional 2 EP to declare a Barrage.

    After the Clash resolves, you may immediately declare a Weapon Move against the same target as a Free Action. This Weapon Move must be currently in your Repertoire and belong to an equipped Weapon Shell.

    Chain Fight Moves

    OFFENSE Flurry 1

    DIE: 6 PWR: 0 DMG: 2

    Afflict Tremor 1.

    Exploit: On 4+ Wins, apply 6 Tremor.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • Apply Tremor on 3+ Wins instead.
    • Increase DIE by 1.

    Rank 2

    • If the target has Tremor, increase DIE by 1.
    • If the target has Tremor, increase DIE by 1.

    Rank 3

    • On Break: apply 12 Tremor instead.
    • Unopposed Dice apply twice as much Tremor.
    OFFENSE Overwhelm 0

    DIE: 5 PWR: 0 DMG: 3

    Exploit: This Move gains Afflict "On Win: deal 1 True Harm to the target."

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • Increase DIE by 1.
    • Gain On Unopposed: apply 2 Tremor.

    Rank 2

    • For each True Harm dealt to the target, apply 1 Tremor.
    • If the target has a status effect and this Move scored at least 1 Win, they take 3 True Harm after this Move resolves.

    Rank 3

    • On 4+ Wins: you may pay 1 EP. If you do, this Move gains its Exploit effect, even if it wasn't your Misdirection Move.
    • Deal 2 True Harm on Win instead.
    DEFENSE Counterstrike 3

    DIE: 4 PWR: 2 DMG: 2

    Exploit: When rolling dice for this Move, each natural 6 rolled adds +1 DIE to the pool. Roll these dice all at once, and they cannot generate additional dice or cause Breaks. Append them to the end of the pool in descending order.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • Decrease EP by 1.
    • Increase DIE by 1.

    Rank 2

    • Natural 6s rolled are immune to Breaks.
    • If this Move deals at least 6 Harm, add an additional 4 Harm to its Gross Harm Pool.

    Rank 3

    • Add additional dice on 5s and 6s instead of just 6s.
    • The extra dice rolled can each add 1 additional die if they roll a natural 6.
    PASSIVE Adrenaline

    If you scored at least 1 Win on a Move triggered by Misdirection on your previous Allegiance Turn, the first Offensive Move you declare this Allegiance Turn gains +1 DIE. Its DIE is capped at 2, and is lowered accordingly.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • If you scored at least 1 Win on a Move triggered by Misdirection on your previous Allegiance Turn, the first Action you take this turn costs -1 EP, to a minimum of 0.
    • Increase the maximum DIE to 3.

    Rank 2

    • Also gain 1 EP.
    • Increase the bonus to +2 DIE.

    Rank 3

    • Whenever you score 4 or more Wins on a single Move, gain 1 EP.
    • If you scored at least 1 Win on a Move triggered by Misdirection on your previous Allegiance Turn, the first Offensive Move this turn also gains +1 if it has at least 4 DIE.

    Chain Hustle Moves

    UTILITY Breach and Clear 2

    Activate this Move to bypass a locked door, window, or physical barrier violently. If this initiates a Fight Scene, you start with an additional Momentum. The GM may rule that a barrier is too hard to bypass, such as a blast door or brick wall.

    TRAIT Well-Trained

    Choose one Weapon Shell. Gain an automatic Boon on any narrative checks that involve brandishing, maintaining, or performing tricks with that Weapon Shell.

    Verdict

    "Strike sure," he said, "or don't strike at all."

    A Verdict Operator is all about precision and Power, overwhelming foes and benefitting from Breaks.

    Verdict Feature

    FEATURE Guillotine

    When you trigger Misdirection, you may spend an additional 2 EP to declare a Guillotine.

    If the new Offensive Move scores at least one Win during the Clash, it deals bonus True Harm equal to its base PWR stat.

    Verdict Fight Moves

    OFFENSE Shatter 2

    DIE: 2 PWR: 3 DMG: 10

    Exploit: increase DIE by 1.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • Add "On Win: the target loses 1 AV for the next Clash"
    • Increase DIE by 1.

    Rank 2

    • Decrease EP by 1.
    • Add Vicious.

    Rank 3

    • Add "Asll Unopposed dice have double DIE"
    • On Break: the target's Armor is treated as 1 Anomaly Tier lower for the purposes of Scaling in this Clash.
    OFFENSE Exploit 1

    DIE: 1 PWR: 4 DMG: 18

    Exploit: Add "On Break: reduce the target's next Move's DIE by 2."

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • Add Bulwark 2.
    • Add "Exploit: add Bulwark 4.

    Rank 2

    • Decrease target's DIE by 3 instead.
    • Increase by 1, but decrease DIE by 8.

    Rank 3

    • Change "On Break" to "On Win."
    • Decrease DIE by 3 instead.
    DEFENSE Parry 1

    DIE: 1 PWR: 4 DMG: 8

    This Move causes a Break if a die is 4+ higher than the opposing die instead of double.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • Add "On Break: gain 2 EP."
    • Add Flow 1.

    Rank 2

    • Cause a Break if a die is 3+ higher instead.
    • Increase :material-burst: DMG by 2.

    Rank 3

    • Decrease EP to 0.
    • Add "On Win: Negate the next X dice in the Clash, where X is your Momentum."
    PASSIVE One Shot

    If you used only one Class or Subclass Move on your last Allegiance turn, increase the DIE of your next Weapon Move by 1.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • This effect also applies if you haven't used a Class or Subclass Move this Allegiance turn.
    • Also add Vicious to the Weapon Move.

    Rank 2

    • You may increase DIE by 3 instead if the Weapon Move is the first Action you take on your Allegiance turn. If you do, you may not use any Class or Subclass Moves this turn.
    • If that Weapon Move causes a Break, deal 3 True Harm to the target.

    Rank 3

    • If that Weapon Move causes a Break, gain 1 additional Momentum.
    • Also increase the DIE of that Move by 50%.

    Verdict Hustle Moves

    UTILITY Heavy Presence 1

    When making a check that involves inspiring someone, coercing someone, or keeping your cool, you may activate this Move to reduce the required Beats for the check by 1/2 your Anomaly Tier (rounded up), to a minimum of 1 Beat.

    TRAIT Threat Detection

    If you spend one minute examining a person, you may ask the GM one of the following questions:

    • Are they armed, even if it's concealed?
    • What's their Anomaly Tier?
    • What is their Armor Tier?
