    Nitro

    He burned so bright, but burnt out so quickly.

    A Nitro excels at using their own body for fuel, sacrificing HP for potent effects. Generate Heat upon taking Harm to cash in for rewards.

    Core Features

    FEATURE Reactor

    Torch X: Suffer X True Harm to activate a Move’s Torch effect. If a Move has multiple Torch costs, you must activate them individually. On Innate Moves, you may Torch 5 to increase a Move’s DMG by 50%, rounded down.

    Heat Stacks: Whenever you take Harm from any source, gain 1 Heat (max 6). Spend 3 Heat to reduce the EP of your next Move by 1, to a minimum of 0.

    Manage your 8-Move Repertoire limit carefully between Rests.

    The first time you would generate Heat during your Allegiance turn, generate +1 Heat.

    Rank 3

    • Generate +2 Heat instead.

    Hustle Moves

    UTILITY Redline 3

    Decrease the required Beats by 1/2 your Anomaly Tier (rounded up), to a minimum of 1 Beat, on a Brawn or Reflex check that involves a burst of physical activity, such as ripping a door off its hinges or leaping a gap.

    UTILITY Withstand 1

    Until the start of the next scene, you may ignore the effects of poisons, drugs, and other toxic substances, as well as severe environmental effects like extreme cold and heat. You cannot fall Unconscious.

    TRAIT Unwavering

    Add dice equal to half your Anomaly Tier (rounded up) on all Checks to resist coercion or intimidation.

    TRAIT Running Hot

    You may choose to only rest for 4 hours. If you do, this counts as a full Rest. If you choose to rest a full 8 hours, you heal an additional 6 HP (total 18).

    Subclasses

    Unlocking your first move in a subclass locks you into that path.

    Inferno

    Blood boiling in his veins, Gregor surged onwards. Nothing could stop his onslaught.

    An Inferno specializes in buffing themselves via self-damage.

    Subclass 1 Feature

    FEATURE Flashpoint

    Spend 2 Heat to add +1 DIE to your next Move. This can be used multiple times on one Move.

    You ignore the -1 PWR penalties of Bloodied and Mortal.

    Inferno Fight Moves

    OFFENSE Spark 3

    DIE: 3 PWR: 2 DMG: 3

    Torch X: This Move gains 1/3 X DIE.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • Decrease EP by 1.
    • Increase DIE by 1.

    Rank 2

    • Add "On Loss: target takes 1 True Harm."
    • Add "On Break: increase X by 6."

    Rank 3

    • Add "Torch X: If you Torched at least 8 this way, gain Area 1. For each additional 8 HP Torched this way, increase Area by 1."
    • This Move gains 1/2 X DIE instead.
    OFFENSE Conflagrate 0

    DIE: 4 PWR: 1 DMG: 3

    Area 2.

    Innate.

    Flow 1.

    Afflict Burn 1.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • Remove Innate.
    • Increase EP by 1. Increase by 1.

    Rank 2

    • Increase to Afflict by 1.
    • Increase DMG by 1.

    Rank 3

    • For each target beyond the first, increase Flow by 1.
    • Increase Afflict by 1.
    DEFENSE Power Through 1

    DIE: 3 PWR: 2 DMG: 0

    On Loss: decrease your AV by 1 for this Clash.

    Innate.

    If this Move scores at least 2 Wins, you may immediately activate an Innate Offensive Move against the attacker. This does not cost an Action Slot, but you must still pay its EP cost.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • On Loss: apply 1 Burn.
    • Reduce enemy DMG by 1/4 (rounded up) for this Clash.

    Rank 2

    • Increase DIE by 1 for each 2 Heat you currently have.
    • You no longer decrease your AV by 1 on Loss.

    Rank 3

    • If you take Harm from this Clash, gain 1 EP and 1 Heat.
    • Decrease the number of Wins needed to activate an Offensive Move by 1.
    PASSIVE Overwhelm

    While you have at least 4 Heat, all Moves gain Pierce 2. If they already had Pierce, increase their Pierce by 2.

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • While you have at least 6 Heat, all Moves cost -1 EP, to a minimum of 0.
    • While you have at least 6 Heat, increase the Pierce effect to 3.

    Rank 2

    • Decrease minimum Heat threshold to 3.
    • Decrease the minimum Heat Threshold to 2. If you trigger this Passive, take 3 True Harm.

    Rank 3

    • While you have at least 4 Heat, all Moves gain "on Break: gain 1 EP."
    • While you have at least 4 Heat, all Torch costs are decreased by 2.

    Inferno Hustle Moves

    UTILITY Cinder-step 1

    Perform a feat of impossible speed, catching something before it hits the ground or crossing a gap before a door closes.

    TRAIT Inner Glow

    While idle, you may choose to emit an aura of heat and light. It prevents those around you from experiencing the effects of minor to moderately cold environments, and provides 5 feet of clear vision as well as 15 feet of dim vision around you.

    Martyr

    He moved heaven and earth just to make sure he and his team made it through. That's what made him special.

    Martyrs specialize in taking Harm to buff their allies' Clashes and checks.

    Subclass 2 Feature

    FEATURE Vent

    The first 2 times each allied Allegiance turn you would gain Heat, you may choose to forfeit that Heat. If you do, target ally gains 1 EP.

    Martyr Fight Moves

    DIE: 3 PWR: 2 DMG: 4

    Torch 5: Add "On Win: target ally heals 2 HP."

    PASSIVE Linked Souls

    Whenever an ally takes part in a Clash, you may Torch 4. If you do, increase their DIE by 25% (rounded up).

    View Upgrades

    Rank 1

    • You may also Torch 4 to give them +1 PWR.
    • If you Torched for an ally's Move and they scored a Break, gain 1 Heat.

    Rank 2

    • You may instead Torch 8 to increase their DIE by 50% (rounded up).
    • Whenever you pay a Torch cost for the first time each turn, gain 1 EP.

    Rank 3

    • The ally heals by half your Torch cost.
    • You may spend any amount of Heat to reduce the Move's EP (3 Heat per 1 EP).

    Martyr Hustle Moves

    UTILITY Shared Spark 1+

    You may spend your own FP to Push an ally's Check, adding that many dice to their roll. If you spend at least 3 FP this way, they also gain an automatic Boon on the Check.

    TRAIT Blood Fuel

    When an ally makes a narrative Check, you may reduce your maximum FP limit by 1 for the remainder of the Chapter. If you do, your ally gains 2 automatic Beats and a Boon on their Check. You cannot activate this if your maximum FP is 0. Your FP limit resets to 6 at the start of a new Chapter.
