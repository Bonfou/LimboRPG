# Combat Economy
Combat in Limbo is primarily resource-driven.
## Action Economy
While the Allegiance shares a "Turn," individuals are limited by their **Action Slots**.
### Action Slots
Everyone has two **Action Slots**

- **Spending Slots:** You may declare one <span class="text-offensive">**Offensive Move**</span> or Action per Slot.
- **Defense is Free:** Using a <span class="text-defensive">**Defensive Move**</span> is reactive and does **not** cost an Action Slot.
### Energy Points (EP)
Moves require energy and concentration.

- **Starting EP:** Everyone begins combat with **3 EP**.
- **Cap:** You can hold a maximum of **6 EP**.
- **Regeneration:** At the start of your Allegiance's turn, you regenerate **1 EP**.
* **Cost:** If you have 0 EP, you cannot use Moves that require an EP cost (:material-poker-chip: EP > 0). You must use Moves with no EP cost or wait to rebuild your pool.

## Character Building
### The Repertoire
You may only bring a limited amount of mental and physical muscle memory into a fight.

* **Limit:** You may have a maximum of **8 Moves** prepared in your Repertoire at any time.
* **Scope:** This includes <span class="text-offensive">**Offensive Moves**</span>, <span class="text-defensive">**Defensive Moves**</span>, and **Passives** (including those from Genetics or Cybernetics).
* **Swapping:** You may reconfigure your Repertoire during a Rest or by visiting a Doctor (for Genetics and Cybernetics).
### The Kit
This represents your consumable items accessible during combat. Using a consumable costs an Action Slot.

* **Limit:** You may have a maximum of **4 items** in your Kit at any time. Consumables don't stack.
* **Scope:** This includes all *equipped* consumable items. You can store unequipped consumable items in your inventory.
* **Swapping:** You may spend an Action Slot to add a new consumable from your inventory to your Kit, provided there's enough space.
