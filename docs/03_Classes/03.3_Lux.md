# Lux
>"What, you think I can't just make one?"

Lux is a battlefield controller that specializes in creating temporary constructs to support its allies and hurt its enemies. Spawn and Shatter Constructs with powerful effects.
## Core Features

{% set feat_text %}
Gain the Manifest Action: Spend 2 EP to create a Construct. You may only have one active at a time; summoning a new one causes the oldest to vanish without effect. Constructs have 15 HP, 5 AV, and share your Anomaly Tier for the purposes of Scaling. If a Construct reaches 0 HP, it vanishes without effect. All Constructs vanish without effect at the end of combat.

Shatter: during your Allegiance Turn or when an enemy targets you, a Construct, or an ally, you may Shatter your active Construct as a 0-EP Action, triggering its Shatter Effect and destroying it. Shattering does not cost an Action Slot.

Constructs:

* Prism Array: Whenever you or an ally scores a Win, Prism Array deals 2 True Harm to any other target. Shatter Effect: Deal 8 True Harm to each target that’s taken Harm this turn.

* Hardlight Wall: When you or an ally would take Harm, you may spend 1 EP to cause the Hardlight Wall to take that Harm instead. Shatter Effect: When you or an ally is targeted by a Move, you may Shatter Hardlight Wall to cancel that Move. All resources spent on that Move other than Action Slots are refunded.

* Focusing Lens: When created, choose a target. All Moves against that target gain +50% DMG (rounded down). You may change targets as an Action (0 Slots, 1 EP). Shatter Effect: Each opponent takes 4 True Harm whenever they take Harm for the remainder of the turn.
