    Move Anatomy

    In the City of Limbo, every ability, biological mutation, and tactical maneuver is represented by a Move Card. While all cards share a unified design, their colors, icons, and Type Badges identify their role in the game and how they interact with your Repertoire.

    Identifying a Move

    A Move Card is defined by several key components that dictate its cost and effect:

    • Type Badge & Color: Located at the top-left, the badge (e.g., OFFENSE, UTILITY) and the card's header color indicate its category.
    • The Cost:
      • Energy Points ( EP): Used for Fight Moves. You regenerate 1 EP at the start of your turn and can hold a maximum of 6.
      • Focus Points ( FP): Used for Hustle Moves. These regenerate at the start of a new Chapter and can hold a maximum of 6.
    • Stats: Offensive and Defensive moves display Dice ( DIE), Power ( PWR), and Damage ( DMG).
    • Keywords: Bolded terms like Flow, Vicious, or Innate that trigger specific rules.

    Fight Moves (The Repertoire)

    Fight Moves represent your combat muscle memory. You may only have 8 Fight Moves equipped in your Repertoire at any time, and they can only be swapped during a Rest.

    Offensive Moves

    Offensive Moves are used during your Allegiance’s Action Phase. Spending an Action Slot allows you to declare one Offensive Move to initiate a Clash against a target.

    Defensive Moves

    Defensive Moves are used reactively when you are the target of a Clash. Using a Defensive Move is free and does not cost an Action Slot. If you have no Defensive Moves in your Repertoire, you roll 0 dice.

    Passive Moves

    Passive Moves provide constant combat benefits. Unlike Offensive or Defensive moves, they do not require a Clash to function, but they do occupy one of your 8 Repertoire slots.

    Features

    Features provide constant combat benefits, similar to Passive Moves. Unlike Passive Moves, however, Features do not take up a Repertoire Slot. They often add mechanics or new systems to the game.

    Hustle Moves (The Toolkit)

    Hustle Moves represent your innate nature and specialized training. You can possess an infinite number of Hustle Moves; they never count against your Repertoire limit.

    Utility Moves

    Utility Moves are active abilities used during narrative play. They typically require the expenditure of Focus Points ( FP) to achieve extraordinary results.
